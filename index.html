Waste Pyrolysis Digital Reactor Simulator

A predictive tool to estimate fuel production from mixed waste using catalytic pyrolysis. This simulator estimates oil yield, gas yield, biochar formation, and environmental benefits.

About the Simulation

This digital reactor model simulates catalytic pyrolysis of mixed waste streams such as plastics, textiles, and biomass. The user can adjust feedstock mass, catalyst loading, reactor temperature, and reaction time to observe predicted product yields and reactor performance.
